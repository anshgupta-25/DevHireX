## 💡 POST TIPS FOR 10K+ IMPRESSIONS

| Tip | Why it works |
|-----|-------------|
| **Post between 8–10 AM IST** (Tue/Wed/Thu) | LinkedIn's algorithm favors early morning content when professionals are scrolling |
| **Reply to EVERY comment** within first 2 hours | High early engagement = algorithm boosts your post to more feeds |
| **First line is the hook** | Only 2-3 lines show before "see more" — "I built a full-stack hiring platform. From scratch. As a student." stops the scroll |
| **Add a carousel or demo video** | Posts with media get 3x more reach than text-only |
| **Tag your mentors** | Their networks (1000s of connections) see your post when they engage |
| **"Drop a 🚀"** | Gives people a low-effort way to react — drives comment count up |
| **Don't edit for 1 hour** | Editing kills reach — LinkedIn deprioritizes edited posts |
| **Share to 2-3 LinkedIn groups** | Manual amplification to relevant audiences |
| **Put the link in first COMMENT** | Links in the main post reduce reach by ~40% |
| **Story > Features** | People connect with your journey, not just what the app does |
